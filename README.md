# Vrinda-Store-Annual-Report-Using-Excel
This Excel dashboard summarizes Vrinda Store's annual performance for 2023, focusing on key metrics: 1)Orders vs. Sales: The dashboard shows monthly trends, with the highest sales and orders in April and May, followed by a decline toward December.2)Order Status: 92% of orders were successfully delivered, indicating efficient logistics.

# Key Components of the Analysis:
1. Overall Sales Data (Vrinda Store Sheet)
Contains detailed order-level data including:
-Order ID, Customer ID, Gender, Age, Age Group
-Order Date, Month, Status, Sales Channel
-Category, Size, Quantity, Currency, Order Amount
-Shipping City, State, Postal Code, Country
This data allows for:
-Trend analysis (monthly/seasonal trends)
-Customer segmentation (age group, gender, location)
-Sales performance comparison (by category, size, platform)

2. Sales Channels Performance (Channels Sheet)
Shows order distribution across different platforms:
-Amazon (35.45%)
-Myntra (23.31%)
-Flipkart (21.58%)
Helps identify which platforms generate the most sales.

3. Customer Demographics (Age and Gender Sheet)
Orders are categorized by gender and age group:
Men:
-Adults: 15.47%
-Seniors: 5.91%
Women:
-Adults: 34.59%
-Seniors: 13.69%
Women form the majority of the customer base.

4. Top Performing States (Top 5 States Sheet)
Highest sales revenue by state:
-Maharashtra: ₹2,982,659
-Karnataka: ₹2,646,358
-Uttar Pradesh: ₹2,104,659
Helps target marketing efforts in high-sales regions.

5. Order Status Insights (Order Status Sheet)
Breakdown of order outcomes:
-Delivered Orders: 28,641
-Cancelled Orders: 844
-Refunded Orders: 517
Helps in analyzing return rates and customer satisfaction.

6. Sales Trends by Month (Order vs Sales Sheet)
Monthly order count and revenue analysis:
-January: 2,702 orders → ₹1,820,601
-February: 2,750 orders → ₹1,875,932
-March: 2,819 orders → ₹1,928,066
Helps in identifying peak sales periods.

7. Gender-based Sales Analysis (Men vs Women Sheet)
Total revenue by gender:
-Men: ₹7,613,604
-Women: ₹13,562,773
Women contribute significantly higher revenue.

# Conclusion & Insights
-Women are the dominant buyers, contributing to over 60% of total revenue.
-Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart are the top-performing platforms.
-Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh generate the highest sales.
-January to March are strong sales months, indicating seasonality.
-Refund & Cancellation rates are low, indicating a stable business model.
